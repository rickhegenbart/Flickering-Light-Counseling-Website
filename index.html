About your counselor

Hope can remain, even when life feels unsteady.

I provide compassionate, collaborative individual counseling for children, adolescents, and adults navigating difficult seasons, important transitions, and the work of creating meaningful change.

The flickering light represents each person—and the hope and resilience that can remain even during periods of uncertainty or struggle. My goal is to offer a safe, supportive environment where you can explore what you are experiencing, understand the patterns affecting your life, and develop practical strategies for moving forward.

Experience grounded in real-world care

I completed my licensure supervision through Yellowstone Academy at Yellowstone Boys and Girls Ranch, where I spent several years supporting children and adolescents who struggled with social and emotional regulation, trauma-related concerns, and challenges at school. Using a trauma-informed approach, I helped young people build coping skills, strengthen emotional awareness, and navigate academic, behavioral, and interpersonal concerns.

For more than two years, I have also worked in private practice with adults across the lifespan, helping clients navigate major life transitions, relationship concerns, and marital communication. My background also includes hospital-based work supporting individuals experiencing acute trauma and significant emotional distress.

Across these settings, my approach draws heavily from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Mindfulness. We work together to recognize unhelpful patterns, develop practical coping strategies, improve communication, and create sustainable change.

“None of us are perfect. We are all just flickers of light in the great flame of humanity. Together we light the world.”

Therapeutic approach

Grounded, practical, and centered on you.

Therapy is a collaborative process. Together, we look at what is happening now, what may be underneath it, and what can help you move forward with greater clarity and confidence.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

CBT helps identify the connections among thoughts, emotions, and actions—then develops practical strategies for change.

Mindfulness

Mindfulness builds awareness of the present moment, helping you respond to difficult experiences with more steadiness and intention.

Collaborative care

Your perspective, strengths, and goals guide the work. Counseling is adapted to what is useful and meaningful for you.

Frequently asked questions

What to know before getting started.

Practical information about services, appointments, and policies.

Who do you work with?

I work with children and adolescents experiencing social and emotional regulation difficulties, trauma-related concerns, and challenges at school. I also work with adults navigating anxiety, depression, major life transitions, relationship concerns, and marital communication.

Do you offer in-person or telehealth appointments?

Appointments are available in person in Billings or through telehealth.

What happens during the first appointment?

The first appointment is an intake session. Intake paperwork is completed, the presenting reason for seeking therapy is discussed, and initial counseling goals are established. This appointment is billed as an intake.

What is your cancellation policy?

A minimum of 48 hours’ notice is required when canceling an appointment. Cancellations made with less notice are billed at half of the scheduled session cost.
